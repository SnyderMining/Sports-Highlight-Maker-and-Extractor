How to Make a Highlight

  1. Load your game video. Choose the footage you want to work with.
  2. Find the play. Pause the video at the moment you want to highlight. Use the frame-step buttons or arrow keys to fine-tune the position.
  3. Add the spotlight. Click on the player in the video, then adjust the highlight size and color.
  4. Trim the clip. Set the start and end points around the play you want to keep.
  5. Export your highlight. The app records the selected section and downloads the finished clip.

Tip: Click a timestamp in Highlight Size Adjust to jump to that spotlight. Use its Remove button to delete just that one, or Remove all to clear every spotlight.

Video stuttering or not playing correctly?

Some videos need to be converted before they will play smoothly. Use the free HandBrake video converter and try these settings:

  • Summary: Set Format to MP4 and check Web Optimized.
  • Video: Set Encoder to H.264 (x264) and select Constant Framerate.
  • Enhanced Settings (under Video): Enter keyint=30:min-keyint=30.
  • Audio: Make sure Source and Codec are both AAC.

Keyboard shortcuts: Space plays or pauses; Left and Right arrows step one frame. Hold Shift with an arrow key to move one second.
